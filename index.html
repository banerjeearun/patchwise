PatchWise

Is this advisory really your problem, and what's the smallest upgrade that fixes it?

What it does

Checks your pinned Python packages against every known advisory, finds the smallest upgrade that clears them (on your release line when one exists), and explains what changes on the way, with sources.

Who it's for

Developers and on-call engineers triaging a security alert who can't take a major upgrade this sprint, like teams on Django 4.2 LTS.

How to use it

  1. Paste exact pins (django==4.2.10) or a requirements.txt.
  2. Optionally name an advisory (CVE / GHSA).
  3. Wait 30–90 s while researchers work in parallel.
Try:
Covers: djangourllib3pillow aiohttpstarlettecryptography · advisory snapshot 2026-11-05 (OSV, PyPI, project changelogs)
Version facts are computed in code from OSV advisory ranges and PyPI releases, never guessed by the model. PatchWise can't see your code, so it can't tell whether you use the vulnerable feature, and it checks direct pins only, not your full dependency tree.
